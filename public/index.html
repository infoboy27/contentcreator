Private growth reporting for short-form content

ContentCreator is an internal analytics and reporting tool built and operated by jfmcss. It connects to TikTok's Login Kit to read basic statistics for accounts the operator owns, and turns that data into private growth reports delivered through Telegram — supporting a personal short-form video workflow that also covers YouTube and Facebook.

ContentCreator is not a public product for third parties. Only TikTok accounts explicitly owned and authorized by the operator are ever connected.

About ContentCreator

ContentCreator supports a small content operation that produces short-form devotional and entertainment videos. The tool automates the parts of that workflow that don't need a human: drafting scripts, generating narration and imagery, assembling vertical video, and — once a video is published — pulling back performance data so the operator can see what's working.

The TikTok integration on this site is one piece of that: it lets the operator link their own TikTok account so ContentCreator can read account-level statistics and summarize them, rather than requiring the operator to check the TikTok app manually.

How it works

1

Authorize

The operator connects their own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit (OAuth). No third-party accounts are connected.

2

Read

ContentCreator reads basic profile information and account statistics made available through the TikTok API for that authorized account.

3

Summarize

The data is compiled into a short, structured growth report — followers, likes, and recent video performance.

4

Deliver

The report is sent privately to the operator via Telegram, on request or on a schedule.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information (username, display name, avatar) for the authorized account
  • Account-level statistics (follower count, likes, video count)
  • Public video metadata for performance review

ContentCreator does not access or request data for any account other than the one explicitly authorized by its owner, and does not sell or share this data with third parties. See the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
