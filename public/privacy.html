ContentCreator Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Operator

ContentCreator is built and operated by jfmcss as a private analytics and automation tool for its own short-form content workflow.

Information We Access

When authorized by the account owner, ContentCreator may access basic profile information, account statistics and public video information from connected platforms, including TikTok via TikTok Login Kit.

How We Use Information

Information is used to create private growth reports, monitor content performance, compare videos and help the owner plan future content.

Sharing

ContentCreator does not sell personal data. Reports are sent only to the account owner or destinations configured by the owner, such as Telegram.

Storage and Security

Credentials are stored in the private automation environment and should be rotated if exposed. Access is limited to the operator's automation stack.

Deletion

The account owner can request removal of connected account data and credentials from the automation environment at any time by contacting the operator.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact jmservicedo@gmail.com.
